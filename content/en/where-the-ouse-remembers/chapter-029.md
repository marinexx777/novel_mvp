title: "— THE SCHOOL BY THE RIVER"
---
Eleanor transformed Arthur's passion into an organisation that could survive Arthur.

They registered as a community arts charity. They applied for local grants, partnered with schools and heritage groups, and created paid evening performances whose income subsidized free classes.

Adrian's townhouse hotel hosted monthly programmes curated by the school.

Not fake medieval feasts.

Real local history, performed by people who lived in the city.

Arthur taught when he could. On difficult days another tutor led while he sat in the front row correcting everyone.

Oliver created music arrangements using guitar and electronic beats.

Arthur hated them.

Then visitors under thirty started coming.

Arthur hated that evidence too.

One afternoon Oliver performed an old comic refrain over a modern rhythm.

Arthur shouted, “You have murdered the tune!”

Oliver shouted back, “At least people came to the funeral!”

The students collapsed laughing.

Arthur tried not to.

Failed.

Eleanor watched from the doorway.

Preservation, she finally understood, was not freezing something.

It was allowing change without losing the thread.
