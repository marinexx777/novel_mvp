title: "— NOAH’S APPEAL"
---
The school’s decision against Noah was reviewed after a whistleblower came forward.

The deputy head had accepted money through a “consultancy” connected to a Price family business.

CCTV showed him entering the exam room before pupils arrived.

The cheating allegation was withdrawn.

The school offered Noah reinstatement.

He refused.

“I’ll go somewhere else.”

Emma supported him.

The family found a sixth-form college in the next county.

Noah’s first term report was excellent.

Her father framed it.

Noah threatened to burn the frame.

Nobody believed him.
