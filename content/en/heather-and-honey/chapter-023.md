title: "— TRIAL"
---
Gavin denied everything.

His lawyers argued that Emma had exaggerated a failed romance and that unrelated officials had acted independently.

The prosecution produced messages.

Payments.

Courier records.

Location data.

Peter’s testimony.

The private investigator’s recordings.

School payments.

The false vehicle registration.

Evidence from the shed fire.

Gavin’s defence weakened piece by piece.

Emma testified for two days.

He stared at her throughout.

She refused to look away.

When she left court, she vomited behind a wall.

Courage, she discovered, often had terrible timing.
