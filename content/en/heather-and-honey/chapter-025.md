---
chapter: 25
title: "— DANIEL’S DECISION"
---
The county offered Daniel a better post after the inquiry.

He declined.

Emma accused him of being insane.

“I thought you wanted to fix government.”

“I wanted to understand it.”

“And?”

“I understand enough to know I can do public work without being employed by the council.”

He joined a regional rural-development foundation.

The salary was lower.

Emma laughed for a full minute.

“You secretly have money. You refuse promotions. You own three good shirts.”

“Four.”

“This relationship is built on deception.”

“Should I leave?”

“No.”

He smiled.

“Good.”
