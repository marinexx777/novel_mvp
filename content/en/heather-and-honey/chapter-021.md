chapter: 21
title: "— THE WARRANTS"
---
Police arrived at three properties before sunrise.

Gavin Price was arrested on suspicion of stalking, coercive control, conspiracy to commit fraud, arson-related offences and witness intimidation.

His father was arrested later over financial matters.

A former police officer was questioned.

The county council announced an independent review.

Gavin’s uncle resigned from committee roles while denying wrongdoing.

The deputy head was suspended.

Rhys Morgan surrendered through a solicitor.

Peter gave evidence.

Emma watched the news from the honey room.

She felt no triumph.

Only exhaustion.

Daniel squeezed her hand.

“Is it over?”

“No,” he said.

She appreciated him for not lying.
