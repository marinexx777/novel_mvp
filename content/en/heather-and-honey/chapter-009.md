title: "— WILD COLONY"
---
Uncle Martin found the feral colony in a rock face above the old sheep track.

Emma, Noah and Martin climbed for an hour carrying ropes, a transfer box and tools.

The bees had been there for years.

Dark comb filled the cavity.

Old honey smelled of heather, herbs, wax and damp stone.

Emma filmed the entire recovery.

Not because it looked pretty.

Because authenticity had become part of the business.

That night she posted a short video.

No filters.

No luxury packaging.

No claims about miracle cures.

Only:

This is where the honey comes from.

Claire reposted it.

Helen reposted it.

Then former colleagues began ordering.

Emma priced small jars high enough to make her nervous.

They sold out.

A chef in Cardiff ordered twelve.

A delicatessen in Bath asked for samples.

Emma stared at the numbers.

The valley had treated its honey as something ordinary because it had always been there.

The city treated traceable scarcity as value.

For the first time since coming home, Emma felt something other than defence.

She felt momentum.
