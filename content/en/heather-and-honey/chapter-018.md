title: "— FIRST KISS"
---
Their first kiss happened after an argument about invoices.

Emma accused Daniel of ordering forty gift boxes under different names to help her sales.

He denied it.

She proved it.

He admitted it.

She was furious.

“I told you not to subsidise me.”

“I bought presents.”

“For forty people?”

“I know many people.”

“You are a terrible liar.”

“So are you.”

“What am I lying about?”

“That you don’t know why I keep coming here.”

Emma stopped.

Daniel stopped.

The room went quiet.

“Say it,” she said.

“I’m in love with you.”

“That was too direct.”

“You asked.”

“I didn’t say answer honestly.”

He smiled.

She kissed him to stop it.

Then stepped back.

“That was a mistake.”

“Obviously.”

She kissed him again.
