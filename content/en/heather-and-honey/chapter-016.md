title: "— THE INVESTIGATOR"
---
Gavin discovered he was being watched.

A private investigator hired by an intermediary had been documenting his meetings.

Gavin’s men caught him.

The investigator panicked and revealed that a local official—Rhys Morgan—had paid for the surveillance.

Rhys had once helped Gavin target Daniel.

Now he feared Gavin.

The network was turning on itself.

Rhys disappeared on sick leave.

Gavin went looking for him.

By then, external investigators had already begun speaking to witnesses.

Gavin still believed his family connections would protect him.

People like Gavin often confused delayed consequences with immunity.
