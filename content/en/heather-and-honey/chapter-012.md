Daniel’s career worsened.

His complaint about the fraudulent vehicle registration triggered an internal review that somehow focused on his conduct rather than the fraud.

He was removed from the valley programme.

Then a senior compliance officer from outside the county contacted him privately.

“Do you have copies?”

“Yes.”

“Everything?”

“Almost.”

“Then stop talking locally.”

Daniel understood.

The case was bigger than one grant.

Planning approvals.

Community loans.

Charity contracts.

School donations linked to governance favours.

The Price family did not own the county.

They did not need to.

They only needed enough people to believe saying no was expensive.

Daniel began building a chronology.

Emma helped without being asked.

Spreadsheets were, after all, what she did before bees.
