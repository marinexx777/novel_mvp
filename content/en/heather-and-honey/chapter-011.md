chapter: 11
title: "— SLOW SEASON"
---
Winter came.

The bees quietened.

Orders did not.

Emma could not scale wild honey indefinitely without destroying the thing customers valued.

So she changed the business.

She bought local honey from small keepers under strict standards, paid above the valley price, tested batches, documented forage areas and separated varieties by season.

Heather.

Woodland.

Orchard.

Old comb.

She named the brand Black Mountain Honey.

Noah built a crude website.

Claire fixed the copy.

Helen introduced Emma to a packaging designer.

The first Christmas gift boxes sold out in four days.

Emma paid her parents rent.

Her father refused it.

She left the money in his coat.

He returned it in her boots.

They continued until Emma’s mother spent it on a new freezer.

Peace through maternal theft.
