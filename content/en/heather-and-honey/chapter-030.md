---
chapter: 30
title: "— THE WEDDING"
---
They married in late summer.

Small church.

Village hall.

Too much food.

No luxury cars.

No press.

No speeches longer than five minutes, by Emma’s order.

Noah ignored the rule.

Helen came from Bristol.

Claire brought forty jars of the expensive fake “wild honey” brand as a joke.

Emma threatened to throw her out.

Peter attended but sat quietly at the back.

Daniel’s colleagues came.

So did several beekeepers who had never worn suits before and spent the reception complaining about the weather.

Emma’s father cried during the vows and denied it afterward.
