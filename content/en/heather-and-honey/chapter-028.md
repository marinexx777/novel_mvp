---
chapter: 28
title: "— PETER"
---
Peter worked for a contractor in Swansea after nobody local trusted him.

He sent Christmas cards.

Emma did not answer the first year.

She answered the second.

By the third, he visited for an hour.

No money.

No favours.

No requests.

He apologised to Noah.

Noah said, “I’m not ready.”

Peter replied, “Fair.”

Emma noticed.

People could change.

That did not create an obligation to hurry toward them.
