chapter: 22
title: "— THE STORY THEY TELL"
---
Reporters arrived.

Some wanted corruption.

Some wanted romance.

Some wanted “the brave beekeeper who defeated a dynasty.”

Emma rejected almost all interviews.

Helen persuaded her to speak once.

Emma sat in front of a camera and said:

“I didn’t defeat anyone. I kept records. Other people told the truth. Investigators did their jobs. My family stopped being silent. That’s less dramatic, but it’s what happened.”

The clip travelled further than the sensational versions.

People trusted it.

Black Mountain Honey sold out for six weeks.

Emma hated the connection.

Then realised she could not control why people arrived.

Only what she sold them when they did.
