title: "— HEATHER BLOOM"
---
Years passed.

Black Mountain Honey never became a supermarket giant.

Emma considered that success.

It became profitable, respected and difficult to imitate.

Customers could scan a code and see the beekeeper, harvest month, forage zone and batch testing.

Noah studied environmental engineering.

He came home each summer and redesigned systems Emma had not asked him to redesign.

Daniel’s foundation helped villages across Wales build small enterprises without selling control to outside investors.

Sometimes he and Emma argued about work at breakfast.

Sometimes they stopped because the bees were swarming.

Life became ordinary in the best possible way.
