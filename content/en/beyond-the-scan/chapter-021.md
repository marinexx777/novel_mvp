title: "— HOPE CENTRE"
---
The Greyfell diagnostic programme expanded into a permanent facility named the Margaret Bennett Centre for Imaging and Access.

Clara resisted the name.

Julian insisted Margaret would have complained theatrically and secretly approved.

The centre did not provide free private medicine. It worked inside the public system, targeting the barriers that still made universal coverage unequal in practice: distance, late referral, lack of specialist interpretation and fragmented follow-up.

Daniel spent a year splitting his time between Northbridge and Greyfell.

Clara joined him.

They married in a small ceremony beside a lake after twelve months of arguing about dates.

Marcus attended.

He gave them an embarrassingly expensive espresso machine.

"This is bribery," Daniel said.

"For what?"

"Future orthopaedic referrals."

Marcus laughed.

For one evening, the old three existed again.
