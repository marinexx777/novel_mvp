chapter: 32
title: "— THE AWARD"
---
The ceremony from Daniel's future finally caught up with him.

He received a national medal for contributions to medical technology and access.

Reporters wanted a story about genius.

Daniel gave them one about systems.

They wanted rivalry.

He gave them uncertainty.

They wanted Marcus reduced to scandal.

He refused.

Afterward, Clara met him outside the hall.

"You know that humility is becoming suspiciously polished," she said.

"I practise in front of mirrors."

"Liar."

"Scientifically unverified allegation."

She took his arm.

They walked into the rain.
